The challenge and opportunity

Farm residue is a resource, not just waste.

Crop residues are sometimes burned or discarded when affordable collection and processing options are unavailable. AgriEcoFree explores a more sustainable way to collect, process and reuse these materials.

🌾

Collect Crop Residues

Connect farmers with local collection points for rice straw, wheat straw and other suitable agricultural residues.

🧪

Process Responsibly

Sort and prepare suitable materials for processing with appropriate technologies and safe handling.

📦

Create Useful Products

Explore biodegradable bags, protective packaging and compostable materials made from suitable crop residues.

A circular model

How AgriEcoFree works

A proposed process to bring farmers, collection centers, processors and buyers closer together.

01

Farmer connects

Farmers share the type, quantity and location of available crop residue.

02

Residue is collected

Collection partners coordinate pickup or drop-off, depending on local availability.

03

Material is processed

Suitable residue is sorted and sent to an appropriate processing unit.

04

Products reach buyers

Manufacturers create useful products that can be offered to interested buyers.
